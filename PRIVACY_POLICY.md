# Namma Thalapathy — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 25 October 2026  
**Contact:** kklabsinfo@gmail.com

Namma Thalapathy is an independent, unofficial fan-community app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vijay, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), or their representatives.

## Information used by the app

**On-device information.** The app stores your saved Vibe Check items, recent public-content caches, a local Home activity count used for your check-in streak, and your daily check-in streak in local app storage. Local activity does not represent the community-wide total. These values are displayed only for your installation; they are not a global visitor count. The app does not currently require account registration or a contact-list permission.

**Shared Total Home Views.** If the publisher configures the optional PHP/MySQL view counter on its existing hosting, opening Home sends a randomly generated event identifier to that server over HTTPS. The server uses a keyed, daily-rotating hash of the connection IP address to limit duplicate or abusive submissions. The raw IP is not stored in the counter database, although normal hosting access logs may contain it. Event identifiers and hashed rate-limiting data are removed after approximately 48 hours when the scheduled cleanup is configured; an aggregate count remains and is shown to all app users. The total counts accepted Home view events, not unique people, and may be affected by abuse controls. Without the configured service, no shared view request is sent and the app does not display a made-up total.

**Optional approximate location.** If you tap **Use Location** and grant the approximate-location permission, the app may read the device's available recent coarse location to rank listed businesses near you. Location is used on-device, is not required to browse the app, and is not uploaded to an Namma Thalapathy server. You can deny or revoke permission through Android settings.

**Public content and external links.** The app downloads public updates, events and business directory data from the public GitHub content repository. Opening an original source or business website takes you to an external website or app, which may receive technical connection information (such as your IP address) and has its own privacy rules.

**Business listing emails.** If you choose to request a listing, the app opens a prefilled email draft in your email application addressed to **kklabsinfo@gmail.com**. Nothing is submitted merely by opening the draft. If you send it, your chosen email provider transfers the business details, contact information, and your sender email address to that inbox so the request can be reviewed. Do not send private information you are not authorized to share. Requests may be retained as reasonably necessary for review, records and correspondence. You can email the contact address above to request deletion of the request information.

**Namma Fan Lounge guest messaging.** When the Lounge is enabled, adults can choose a nickname and join without providing their phone number, email address or real name. The app generates a random guest credential, encrypts the bearer token in Android Keystore-backed local storage and stores only its hash in the community database. The service stores guest nickname, agreement and age self-attestation timestamps, public chat text, reply links, emoji reactions, guest blocks, moderation reports and moderation outcomes. Other permitted participants can see your nickname, published messages, replies and public reaction totals. Moderators can review reported or removed material to enforce community standards. Personal contact details and explicit sexual or abusive text are prohibited in the chat and screened server-side; automated filtering cannot guarantee that every unsafe message will be stopped.

**Safety and anti-abuse.** The community backend may process network connection information including an IP address to enforce posting, reporting and guest-creation limits. The database keeps a keyed one-way hash for throttling rather than a raw IP record; normal web-server security logs may separately contain IP addresses. Rate-limit rows are designed for deletion on a recurring schedule. Guests can report messages and block participants inside the app. Reports and moderation actions are kept as reasonably necessary to address safety complaints.

**Leaving the Lounge.** Guest participants can choose **Leave** in the Lounge and confirm deletion. This revokes that guest's access token and removes the text of their public posts from normal display. Records needed for prior abuse-report handling or legal compliance may remain in a de-identified or limited form. Rejoining creates a new guest identity. Questions or additional privacy requests can be directed to the contact email above.

**Sharing.** Sharing a card uses the Android share sheet. Only when you choose a destination app is the content provided to that destination, subject to its privacy rules. The original article source URL is not included in the share caption.

## Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform components. Public-release ad requests are disabled while only Google's test IDs are configured. If production advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device identifiers, approximate IP-based location, consent preferences, ad performance and interaction data as described in their policies and subject to applicable consent choices.

- Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Users may be shown a Google consent form where required. If you do not permit the required ad requests, the app can continue to be used for its core public-content features.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Local saved items, local activity counts and streak values stay on your device until you clear this app's storage or uninstall the app; temporary cached files may be removed by Android earlier. Lounge guest credentials are stored locally with Android KeyStore-backed encryption and server guest records remain until the guest leaves or they are removed as part of moderation or a data request. Public messages, reaction totals and moderation records may remain longer than local app storage, according to safety, backup and legal retention requirements. This app does not maintain a remote account profile for these values. Any business-listing email you send can be requested for deletion by contacting **kklabsinfo@gmail.com**, subject to lawful retention requirements. Third-party services apply their own retention rules.

## Security, children and changes

The app uses HTTPS for public content requests. Standard Android app-private storage protects local records under the operating system's security model. No transmission or storage method is guaranteed completely secure.

The Lounge is intended only for adults aged 18 or older and requires an age declaration and agreement to the community rules before accessing the public room. Child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, harassment and content that endangers minors are strictly prohibited. A self-declaration is not conclusive age verification; the operator must also apply any relevant Google Play minor-access controls before enabling the Lounge publicly.

We may update this policy when app functionality or third-party data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a new effective date.
