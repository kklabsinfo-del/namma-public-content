# Namma Thalapathy — Child Safety Standards

**Contact for child-safety concerns:** kklabsinfo@gmail.com

Namma Thalapathy is an independent, unofficial community application. Its common Fan Lounge is designed for adults aged 18 and over; users must confirm eligibility and accept its rules before joining. The operator must apply applicable Google Play age-restriction controls before making the Lounge publicly accessible.

## Zero tolerance

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, sexual solicitation involving minors, attempts to obtain minors' personal contact information, and promotion or links to such material. Explicit sexual content, threats, harassment, hate and sharing personal contact details are not allowed in the Lounge.

## Prevention and moderation

The Lounge uses server-side text validation to restrict explicit adult content, abusive terms, phone numbers, contact information and links. Guests cannot privately message others or upload images, videos or audio. Automated checks can miss harmful behavior; moderators review reports and can hide content, mute or ban guests and pause posting immediately.

## How to report

In the Lounge, open a message's **Options → Report**, choose the relevant safety category and submit. Guests can also select **Block guest** to stop seeing that participant's messages. For urgent or sensitive child-safety concerns, email **kklabsinfo@gmail.com** with relevant message identifiers and a brief explanation. Do not email or redistribute CSAM.

## Our response

The moderation team reviews and acts on credible safety reports, removes prohibited content, prevents further posting where necessary, retains limited evidence as required by law, and escalates suspected child sexual abuse to appropriate authorities when legally required. The service maintains administrative audit records.

## Guest participation

No email, phone number or real identity is required for an ordinary guest nickname. This does not mean that users are allowed to share personal contact details. A guest may leave the Lounge and remove the public content they posted.

These standards are part of the service's community rules and may be revised as safety practices and legal requirements evolve.
