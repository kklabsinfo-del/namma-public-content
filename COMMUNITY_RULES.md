# Namma Fan Lounge — Community Rules

**Adults only (18+).** Namma Fan Lounge is a common public discussion space for cinema fans. Every participant must confirm eligibility and accept these rules before joining.

1. Treat people respectfully. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, coercion, impersonation or slurs.
2. No pornography, explicit sexual content, grooming, sexual exploitation or sexual abuse of minors.
3. No mobile numbers, WhatsApp contacts, personal email addresses, social handles, websites, home addresses or requests to move a conversation privately.
4. No scams, spam, phishing, private solicitation, paid meetups, political fundraising or requests for personal data.
5. Hide film spoilers inside `||double bars||` so readers can decide when to reveal them.
6. Use the **Report** and **Block guest** options if someone violates the rules. Do not retaliate publicly.
7. The owner can remove messages, pin notices, pause posting, mute or ban guests as needed to keep the room safe.

**Privacy and guest deletion:** The room uses pseudonymous guest credentials and stores real message and reaction records. A guest can delete their own messages or choose **Leave** to revoke the guest credential and remove public posts. Limited safety and moderation records may remain when required.

See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and [Child Safety Standards](CHILD_SAFETY_STANDARDS.md).
